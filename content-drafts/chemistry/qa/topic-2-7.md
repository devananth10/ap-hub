| 001 | PF5 AX5 trigonal bipyramidal; SF4 AX4E seesaw; ClF3 AX3E2 T-shaped. All five-domain arrangements; equatorial lone pairs minimize 90° interactions in the simple model. Other sequences use wrong domain counts or ignore lone pairs. |

| 005 | XeF4 AX4E2 with opposite lone pairs is square planar; opposite bond dipoles cancel. Not tetrahedral, square pyramidal, or seesaw. |
| 006 | HC≡CH: two C–H sigma plus one C–C sigma and two pi; each carbon two domains/sp. Alternatives omit sigma, count triple components all as sigma, or miscount pi. |
| 007 | Twisting destroys parallel p overlap, giving a pi rotation barrier; not free cylindrical symmetry, absence of sigma, or unchanged p alignment. A finite barrier does not mean motion is impossible at any energy. |
| 008 | CH3–C≡N: methyl carbon four directions/sp3/tetrahedral; nitrile carbon two/sp/linear. Labels apply locally rather than to the entire molecule. |
| 009 | Corresponding C≡C is shorter and has higher total bond energy than C–C. No exact factor of three; one sigma plus two pi components are not three identical sigma bonds. |
