# Topic 5.4 — Elementary Reactions

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Eleven original questions; **human scientific review and owner approval pending**. Exact ID `topic-5-4`; not imported.

Authority: [College Board CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-chemistry-course-and-exam-description.pdf), Fall 2024, accessed 2026-10-17, printed p.98 / PDF105. LO5.4.A/EK .1–.2: elementary rate laws from participating-particle stoichiometry; three-or-more-particle simultaneous events rare. Suggested skill5.E. Elementary assumptions are explicitly supplied, especially for real-species examples. No full mechanism deduction, steady-state/pre-equilibrium approximation, collision-energy distribution or solvent-kinetic theory is required. Molecularity counts reactant particles, not atoms inside molecules or number of species names.

Separate event-count/rate-convention pass produced D/B/A/C/D/A/C/B/C/D/B; positions2/3/3/3. Science script checks rate factors, elementary exponent counts, actual formula balances, and differing observed-versus-proposed H2 powers. It cannot prove any real mechanism is elementary; the prompts make that a premise or a tested hypothesis. "Rare" is not "impossible."
