Reviewed 2026-10-18. Eleven original draft questions; **human scientific review and owner approval pending**. Exact preserved ID `reactions`. Not imported. The unusual title "Introduction for Reactions" is the official CED wording, not a generated correction to "to".

Authority: [College Board CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-chemistry-course-and-exam-description.pdf), Fall 2024, accessed 2026-10-17, printed p.78 / PDF85. LO 4.1.A identifies evidence of physical and chemical changes. EK .1 includes phase changes and mixture formation/separation as physical changes without transformation into different substances; EK .2 identifies new substances and possible heat/light/gas/precipitate/color evidence. Suggested skill 2.B: hypotheses/predicted experimental results. Mixture proportions may change physically, so the bank explicitly distinguishes constituent identity from mixture composition. Balanced/net ionic equations, microscopic inter- versus intramolecular changes (4.4), stoichiometry, and reaction-type algorithms are not the central tasks here.

No numerical calculation is required by this set; the general science script remains passing for earlier numerical sets. Qualitative identity-based classification was checked manually rather than claiming a calculation proves it.
