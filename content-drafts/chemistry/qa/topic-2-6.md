# Topic 2.6 — Resonance and Formal Charge

Status: eleven original questions, **pending human scientific review and owner approval**. Exact topicId `topic-2-6`; not registered in the app.

Authority: [College Board CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-chemistry-course-and-exam-description.pdf), Fall 2024, accessed 2026-10-17, printed p.50 / PDF57. LO 2.6.A; EK .1 equivalent resonance contributors and structural predictions, .2 octet/formal-charge criteria, .3 limitations for odd-electron species; suggested skill 6.C. Prior knowledge: valid Lewis diagrams, valence counts, electronegativity. Exclude quantitative resonance energies, molecular-orbital diagrams, hybrid-orbital derivations, and treating resonance as nuclear motion or rapid switching between separate molecules.

Prompts/options were read separately after drafting without displayed keys, then calculations and explanations were compared. Derived keys B/C/A/D/B/A/C/B/D/A/C match the bank. A/B/C/D = 3/3/3/2. Every distractor is addressed in its explanation.

| Suffix | Separate science/alternative check |
| --- | --- |
| 001 | Equivalent O3 bonds fit delocalization; not a mixture, literal contributor switching, or exchanged nuclei. Usual O3 formal charges are 0/+1/−1 in the drawn ordering. |
| 002 | Carbonate diagram charges C0/double O0/single O−1/single O−1. All alternative sums can equal −2, so each atomic assignment must be checked. |
| 003 | NO3− average bond order (2+1+1)/3=4/3, not 1.5, all-double, or fixed unequal bonds. This is an equal-contributor model, not a time average of switching drawings. |
| 004 | OH−: O formal charge 6−6−1=−1; H0. Alternatives confuse total occupancy with assigned electrons, allocate both bonding electrons, or reverse sign. |
| 005 | NO2 total 17 is odd: at least one unpaired electron; all-complete-octet paired model fails. Cannot discard/add an electron while keeping the species or invoke nitrogen d orbitals. |
| 006 | OCN− I gives −1/0/0; II gives 0/0/−1. Both 16-electron octet structures; I puts negative formal charge on more electronegative O. This preference does not assert a single contributor is the entire structure. |
| 007 | Coupled lone-pair/bond-pair movement preserves 18 electrons, charge −1, nuclei, and octets. Rearranged nuclei, changed electron count, and page rotation do not give another resonance contributor. Prompt explicitly specifies all lone pairs. |
| 008 | Expanded Lewis SF6: S charge 6−0−6=0; all F0, total 48 electrons, S12. Not a d-hybridization claim. Wrong answers omit/overcount bond shares or impose four shares regardless of the supplied diagram. |
| 009 | CO triple bond and one lone pair each: C−1/O+1, ten electrons, both octets. Net neutrality is not zero charge on every atom; electronegativity alone cannot replace bookkeeping. |
| 010 | Correct formal-charge sum is necessary, not sufficient to select a model; local occupancies and other criteria still matter. Large charge magnitudes are not automatically favorable. |
| 011 | Proposed nitrate uses 24 electrons but N has 10 surrounding electrons. Reject expanded second-period N even though formal-charge separation is reduced. Single-bonded O still has its octet. |
