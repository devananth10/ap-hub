# AP Calculus AB curriculum audit

Verified 2026-10-19 from College Board’s current CED (2026 copyright; original effective-fall-2020 cover), the linked course page and fall-2026 clarifications. The older standalone Course at a Glance retains earlier titles/weightings; the current CED takes precedence. This is a curriculum reference, not a student resource import. No College Board questions or sample activities were copied.

**81 AB topics × 11 original MCQs = 891 questions.** Unit counts: 16 / 10 / 6 / 7 / 12 / 11 / 7 / 12.

Exclude BC-only 6.11 integration by parts, 6.12 linear partial fractions, 6.13 improper integrals, 7.5 Euler’s method, 7.9 logistic models, 8.13 arc length, and Units 9–10. Preserve gaps in official numbering: AB includes 6.14, 7.6–7.8. Epsilon–delta proofs are not assessed. New exam timing/counts do not add course content.

2026 clarifications: EVT requires continuity on a closed interval and guarantees at least one maximum/minimum; differential equations may have infinitely many solutions (not every equation must).

Eight previous placeholder number/ID pairs are retained for saved routes and data. Their display names are reconciled with the CED. No earlier Calculus question/draft files were found in this repository; unavailable work elsewhere is not assumed never to have existed. Inspect any recovered files before importing or replacing content.
